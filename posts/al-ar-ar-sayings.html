Al-Ṣarṣarī sayings

August 5, 2025 | Ahl-Al-Sunnah
Al-Ṣarṣarī (RA) said also:

“Seek reunion with the beloved if he turns away —
perhaps, by your noble kindness, he may return.

Remain loyal to love in one’s absence,*
for the noble is the one who guards secrets when unseen.

Do not joke excessively, and be a man of dignity —*
much in silence, god-fearing, and refined.

Do not envy, nor bear hatred, and purify —*
your tongue from slander and backbiting.

For if you rise to act upon this advice —*
you will have settled in a lush meadow of piety.”
