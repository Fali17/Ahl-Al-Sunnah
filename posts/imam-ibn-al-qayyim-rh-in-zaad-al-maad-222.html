Imam Ibn Al Qayyim (rh) in Zaad Al Maad (/222)

2024-11-02 Essay
Then he places the right hand on the back of the left hand above the wrist and forearm. There is no authentic report about their placement but the famous one is mentioned from Ali bin Abi Talib RA that he said:

The Sunnah is to place the hand on the hand in prayer below the navel Ibn Abi Shaybah said: The Sunnah is what is narrated from the Prophet ﷺ and it is established that he used to place his right hand on his left in prayer. Abu Ishaq Al Jawzjani said: As for what about above the navel and below it I do not know it from the Prophet ﷺ except that Ali RA said It's from the Sunnah in the obligatory prayer to place the right hand on the left below the navel.
