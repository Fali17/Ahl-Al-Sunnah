“al-Mawāhib al-Ladinnīyyaħ”, ʾImām ʾIbrāhīm al-Bājūrī ash-Shāfiʿī does Tawassul through the rank of the Prophet ﷺ for his closing Duʿāʾ.

October 19, 2024 | Ahl-Al-Sunnah
"And this compilation, may God have mercy on him, was concluded by highlighting two significant points: an encouragement to perfect the science of Hadith, increasing in it, and exerting effort in acquiring it. He concluded it with what is commonly found in most books of Hadith: 'Indeed, actions are judged by intentions.'


God has made the beginning and the end of this work the best, by the grace of the Prophet, peace and blessings be upon him, and his family and companions."

"To the esteemed gentlemen, may God unite us and them in the abode of peace, with peace.

All praise is due to God, the Lord of the Worlds, and He is my sufficiency and the best guardian. There is no power and no strength except through God, the Most High, the Magnificent.

It was a privilege for whoever compiled this book ..."
